RFM Scoring Methodology

The RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) model partitions your active database into percentile quintiles:

  • Recency (R): Days lapsed since the customer's last order. More recent purchasers receive higher ranks (1-5).
  • Frequency (F): Total lifetime transaction count. Customers with frequent purchases score higher.
  • Monetary (M): Cumulative gross currency spent. Top spenders rank in the highest quintile.
